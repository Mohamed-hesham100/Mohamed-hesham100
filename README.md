## About

I'm a Full-Stack Developer who builds secure, production-ready web applications and deploys them myself on Linux servers.

I work as a freelance developer and have delivered 5+ production applications. My main projects are two e-commerce platforms for the Saudi and Egyptian markets, with local payment gateways and shipping carriers, and Secrela, a B2B platform for protecting company secrets.

I'm a Computer Science student at EELU (expected 2027), open to full-time opportunities, including relocation.
